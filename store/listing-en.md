## Full description
**For the days you need each other most.**

Kenaret is a cycle tracker built for two. One experience is for the person who tracks her cycle, another is for her partner. The goal is awareness and better communication, never stereotypes: every experience is different, and all wording is tentative ("may", "for some people").

**For her**
• Cycle day, days until the next period and the estimated phase at a glance
• An estimated ovulation window (an estimate, never a certainty)
• One-tap period start and end logging
• 10 symptoms with severity, plus private notes
• Monthly calendar (Jalali in Persian, Gregorian in English)
• Cycle statistics and an editable history
• 30 self-care tips

**For her partner**
• Sees only what she chooses to share
• "How can you help today?" and "Better to avoid"
• 30 daily tips for being supportive

**Pairing without a server**
A short code such as KNT-7Q4M-2XA connects two people with no internet and no account. She decides exactly what is shared. Symptoms and notes are never shared.

**Reminders**
Separate wording for her and her partner, a time you choose (default 08:30) and a private mode that hides sensitive details. Lock-screen notifications never show details.

**Privacy**
• No sign-up or login
• All data stays on your phone
• The app has no internet permission at all
• No ads, no trackers
• Delete all data with one tap

**Look and feel**
Persian (right-to-left, Persian digits, Jalali calendar) and English, bundled Vazirmatn font, light and dark themes.

**Medical note**
Information and predictions are approximate and may differ for each person. Kenaret is not a diagnostic tool or a method of contraception, and never suggests medication or doses. If you have severe, persistent or unusual symptoms, talk to a doctor.
